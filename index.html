How long does SEO take to show results?
Most websites see early movement in 6–10 weeks, with stronger ranking and traffic growth over 3–6 months. Anyone promising #1 in a week is selling risk, not SEO. I prioritise quick wins first so you see progress early.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes — I work remotely with clients worldwide. Communication is in clear, professional English, with regular updates and reports you can actually understand. You can reach me by phone or on LinkedIn.
Are your backlinks safe for my website?
Yes. I only place links on real, niche-relevant websites with genuine traffic. No PBNs, no link farms, no automated spam — the kind of links that survive algorithm updates.
What do AEO and GEO actually mean for my business?
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content so it gets chosen as the direct answer in featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand cited and recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini. Together, they put you where customers are now searching first.
What do you need from me to get started?
Just your website URL. I'll run a free audit and send a clear roadmap. If it makes sense, we start — if not, you keep the audit and the insights, no pressure.
Do you provide reporting?
Yes — transparent reporting on work completed, links built, keyword movement and traffic. You'll always know exactly what was done and what's planned next.
